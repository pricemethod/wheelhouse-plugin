## Rules that every screen follows
- **Everything you can tap is big enough to tap** (about the size of a fingertip) and says what it does.
- **Every screen has three states:** loading (something is on the way), empty (nothing here yet, and what to do), and trouble (it did not work, and what to try). Use the same shared pieces for these everywhere; do not invent a new one per screen.
- **Color is never the only signal.** Anything shown in a color also has a word, a shape or a symbol.
- **One look for each thing.** Buttons, cards, headings and messages are made once and reused.
- **Plain words.** Messages say what happened and what to do next, with no technical terms.
