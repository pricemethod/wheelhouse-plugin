## Style rules
- **Short sentences, plain words.** If a word needs explaining, explain it the first time or use a simpler one.
- **Say the point first,** then the support.
- **Numbers always say what they count, over what period, and where they came from.**
- **One name per thing,** taken from the glossary. Do not rotate synonyms.
- **Examples are invented or cleared for use;** never real personal details.
