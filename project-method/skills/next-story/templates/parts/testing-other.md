## How we check documents, reports and processes

1. **Checklist from the brief:** turn the brief's audience and success definition into a short checklist; every draft is read against it.
2. **Facts and numbers:** every claim, figure, name and date is checked against its source, and the source is noted.
3. **Fresh-eyes read:** someone who was not involved reads it as the intended audience and says where they got lost.
4. **Review rounds:** each round has a named reviewer, a date and a list of changes asked for; changes are made or declined in writing.
5. **Try the process:** for an automation or a procedure, run it once on harmless sample material and watch every step before using it for real.
6. **Before handing over:** read it once more from the top, check formatting where it will actually be seen (printed, in the email, on the page), and write down what was and was not checked.
