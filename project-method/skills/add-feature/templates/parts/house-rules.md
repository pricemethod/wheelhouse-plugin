## House rules (they apply in every session)

1. **The files are the memory.** Claude starts every session with no memory of the last one. What exists in this folder is the only continuity. Nothing is built "from memory of an earlier chat".
2. **Decide before building.** Open questions are written down, answered, and recorded in the plan's decisions table. A scope decision is never left implicit.
3. **One story at a time.** Each story is small enough to finish and check in one sitting. Finish it, test it, log it, then stop or continue.
4. **Stop at the review gates.** Where the plan says STOP FOR REVIEW, Claude ends with a summary, shows how to look at the result, and waits for a clear yes before going on.
5. **Ask before anything outward-facing or irreversible.** Sending, publishing, deleting, spending money, or changing real data needs a fresh yes each time. An earlier yes does not carry to the next action.
6. **Report honestly.** If a check failed or was skipped, say so plainly. Never describe unfinished or unchecked work as done.
7. **Log every session, append-only.** Every working session ends with an entry in `BUILD_LOG.md`. Earlier entries are never edited or deleted.
8. **The owner owns the plan.** Claude never changes the plan on its own. When the plan needs to change, Claude writes the exact change in the log under "Plan updates needed", explains it in plain words, and makes it only after the owner says yes to that exact change; then it adds a "Plan updated" entry to the log saying what changed.
9. **No secrets in chat.** Passwords, keys and tokens live in a private file that is never shared or committed. Claude tells you where it goes and never asks you to paste one.
10. **Protect personal details.** Names, phone numbers, addresses, door or lockbox codes and similar details in the project's files are never printed in the chat or the log; work on a copy with them removed where possible, and keep them out of snapshots.
11. **Write down what bites.** When something surprising goes wrong (a limit, a quirk, a stale setting), it is added to the rules so it cannot bite twice.
