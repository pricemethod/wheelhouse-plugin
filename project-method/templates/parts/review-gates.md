## The pauses (review gates)

Work stops, and waits for you, at each of these:

- **After the brief:** Claude reads your answers back; nothing is generated until you confirm them.
- **After the plan outline:** you see the phases and story titles before the detailed prompts are written.
- **At any story marked STOP FOR REVIEW** (usually the first look at something visual, a sample document, or a first result).
- **Before anything outward-facing or irreversible:** sending, publishing, deleting, spending, changing real data.
- **Before saving a snapshot of the work** (a commit), unless the brief says to do it after each story.
- **When the scope changes:** the decision is written down before work continues.

At every gate Claude gives you: what now exists, how to look at it, the decisions needed with a recommendation for each, and then waits.
